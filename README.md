## 📖 Project Overview

Modern supply chains generate massive amounts of operational data. However, businesses often struggle to transform this information into actionable insights for inventory planning, logistics optimization, and profitability improvement.

This project develops an end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics solution using the DataCo Supply Chain Dataset to identify operational bottlenecks, forecast demand, optimize inventory allocation, and support executive decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The company faces several operational challenges:

- Declining profitability
- High inventory costs
- Delivery delays
- Limited demand forecasting
- Poor customer visibility
- Lack of optimization-driven decision making

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Improve profitability
- Analyze customer behavior
- Evaluate logistics performance
- Forecast future demand
- Optimize inventory allocation
- Build executive dashboards
- Generate business recommendations

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Revenue remained stable before declining significantly during late 2017.
- A small number of products generated the majority of revenue.
- Customer segmentation identified high-value customers suitable for retention campaigns.
- Delivery delays varied significantly across markets.
- Optimization models identified high-profit products and markets for inventory prioritization.
